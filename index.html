About Me

Full Stack Developer (React · Python · WordPress)

I’ve been building web applications for just over 10 years now, so I know what works and what turns into technical debt later. Most of my day‑to‑day work is in React on the frontend and Python (Flask / FastAPI) on the backend, but I also inherited a few large WordPress sites that I’ve kept running smoothly for years.

With React, I don’t just write components – I think about state management, performance, and how the UI will scale when the client suddenly gets 10x more users.

Python is where I solve the real logic: data processing, REST APIs, background tasks, and occasionally automating things that clients didn’t even know could be automated. On the WordPress side, I’ve built custom themes from scratch, optimized database queries, and locked down sites that kept getting hacked.

I’m the person clients call when their “simple” plugin stack breaks after an update – and I usually fix it within an hour.

I’ve worked with startups, agencies, and small business owners, so I understand tight budgets and vague requirements – I’ll ask the right questions before writing a single line of code. If you hire me, you’re not getting a junior who needs hand‑holding; you’re getting someone who shows up, solves problems, and delivers working software.
